# Open questions, and the RFCs they need

Informative. The unresolved design questions of the protocol family, in priority order, each with the RFC that would address it. No RFC listed here exists yet: the list says what is needed, not what is decided. Numbers are assigned when an RFC is opened.

Priority is by how much a relying party can be misled today, then by how many other questions wait on the answer.

## A. A result that can be relied on for more than it establishes

These are gaps in the formats. No clarification closes them; each needs a new version ([RFC 0001](../rfcs/0001-standards-process.md), invariant 2).

| #   | Question                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | RFC needed                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A1  | **A verification without the relying party's pins can report a supported decision with no key involved.** A package carries its own contract, and expectations are optional. Should a verifier be required to say, in its result, that the contract was not the relying party's, and should a required decision fail without pins? | _Relying-party pins are required for an established decision._ A required report member, so that every implementation behaves the same                                                               |
| A2  | **Key validity is evaluated at a time the signer asserts.** A holder of a revoked key can backdate. In claim registration the registry's signed time of receipt is available and is not used for the claimant's key                                                                                                                | _Key validity at authenticated time._ For claim registration, the receipt's time. For statements, see B1                                                                                             |
| A3  | **A completeness statement names neither a claim nor a set of records.** Whoever assembles a package can leave a record out after the source signed. One source can close a kind accepted from two                                                                                                                                 | _Completeness bound to what it covers_, by a stream position where the source's statements are chained and anchored, by a digest of the record set otherwise; and closure per source                 |
| A4  | **A package's claim and manifest are unsigned, and its issuer signature is optional.** Integrity checks show self-consistency. Nobody is bound to the claim                                                                                                                                                                        | _WVP v0.3_: the claimant signs the claim, the decision is established only under a pinned issuer key, the evidence commitment includes the claim. It should carry A1 and A3, and must not wait on B1 |

## B. Missing building blocks

| #   | Question                                                                                                                                                                                                  | RFC needed                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| B1  | **Nothing authenticates the time or the end of a stream of statements.** A chain shows that nothing is missing inside it, and nothing about its tail or when it was written                               | _Anchoring a stream head at a registry._ A new registry entry. Open: who may anchor, since an anchor obliges nobody but costs the registry and reveals a tenant and a stream name |
| B2  | **A registry can show different logs to parties that never compare heads, and inclusion is shown by handing over a segment.** Signed heads make equivocation provable once two heads meet, and not before | _A Merkle log with witness-cosigned heads._ The largest change in this list. It should follow B1, once anchors show the volume                                                    |
| B3  | **A contract cannot require how its evidence was captured.** A self-reported record and one observed by an independent party satisfy a requirement equally                                                | _Minimum assurance per requirement._ Open, and decisive: assurance must be a function of the evidence that every verifier recomputes, not a report a verifier trusts              |
| B4  | **The contract language has no arithmetic, aggregation or durations.** Totals and deadlines are then a source's word                                                                                      | _A new language version._ It needs decimal semantics, a work bound that counts only relevant facts, and the formal definition extended with it                                    |
| B5  | **Relations and records have no container.** Work relation v1 says what a reader concludes and not how the objects travel together                                                                        | _A history container_, once there are two producers to learn from                                                                                                                 |

## D. The process itself

| #   | Question                                              | What it needs                                                                                      |

## Suggested order

1. A1 and A2 for claim registration: small, and they close the two gaps a relying party is most likely to fall into.
2. C3: with C1 resolved, it is what stands between the existing specifications and independent implementation.
3. B1, because A2 for statements, A3 and A4 all build on it.
4. A4 (WVP v0.3), carrying A1 and A3, with anchors optional so that it does not wait on a registry.
5. C4 and C5, then B2, B3, B4 and B5 as there is experience to design them from.
