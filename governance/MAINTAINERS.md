# Maintainers

The people who review, decide and merge under [RFC 0001](../rfcs/0001-standards-process.md).

| Maintainer | GitHub                         | Affiliation              |
| ---------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Zin Nam    | [@zy4](https://github.com/zy4) | Krops GmbH (the steward) |

There is one maintainer, from the steward. RFC 0001, section 12.3, says what that means for decisions made in this period, and that adding maintainers from outside the steward is recommended as soon as there are implementers outside it.

Maintainers are appointed by the steward, on the maintainers' proposal, by a pull request to this file.
